What I wanted to understand

When is a good score a bad clue?

I started with two features: one that stays useful, and one that is easier to trust during training. The interesting part is the disagreement group. A high average can conceal how badly the model treats those examples.

This is a small controlled experiment. The balanced-data control changes the training distribution. The stable-only control assumes we already know which feature is stable. Neither is a general solution to distribution shift.
